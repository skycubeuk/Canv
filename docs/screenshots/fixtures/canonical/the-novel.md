# Chapter One

The lighthouse keeper had not slept in three days. He sat at the rim of the
balcony with the sea wind rasping at his coat, watching the bone-white beam
swing out across the water and find nothing.

He thought of his daughter. He thought of the letter folded in his
breast pocket — three lines, no signature — and how the paper had warmed
against him until it felt like another small living thing.

The light turned. The dark turned with it.
